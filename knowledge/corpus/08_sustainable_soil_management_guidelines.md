# FAO Voluntary Guidelines for Sustainable Soil Management (VGSSM)

**Source Authority:** Food and Agriculture Organization of the United Nations (FAO Global Soil Partnership)  
**Publication:** FAO VGSSM Framework / World Soil Charter  
**Topics:** Soil Degradation Prevention, Erosion Control, Nutrient Management, Soil Biological Conservation  

## 1. Principles of Sustainable Soil Health Stewardship
Sustainable soil management maintains or enhances the ecosystem functions and biodiversity of soil without causing structural degradation, groundwater contamination, or salinization.

### Ten Core Pillars of Soil Health Stewardship:
1. **Minimizing Soil Erosion:** Maintaining continuous ground cover (cover crops, mulches, crop residues) to shield topsoil aggregates against droplet kinetic impact and wind detachment.
2. **Enhancing Soil Organic Matter:** Returning organic residues, applying composted organic manures, and integrating green manure rotations to build permanent soil humus.
3. **Fostering Soil Nutrient Balance:** Avoiding over-application of synthetic chemical fertilizers that cause soil acidification, secondary salinization, or eutrophication of surrounding water bodies.
4. **Preventing Soil Salinization & Sodification:** Employing efficient irrigation techniques (drip systems) and scheduling leaching events only when required by salt accumulation.
5. **Mitigating Soil Compaction:** Avoiding heavy machinery operations when soil moisture exceeds field capacity, and cultivating deep-rooted taproot cover crops (e.g., Radish, Mustard) for biological subsoiling.
6. **Protecting Soil Biodiversity:** Minimizing excessive chemical fumigants and pesticides that decimate earthworm populations, beneficial mycorrhizal fungi, and nitrogen-fixing bacteria.
7. **Soil pH Buffering:** Regularly monitoring soil pH and applying balanced neutralizing amendments (lime or gypsum) to preserve root enzyme functions.
