# FAO EcoCrop & ICAR Compendium: Crop Ecological Growth Envelopes and Soil Compatibility

**Source Authority:** Food and Agriculture Organization (FAO EcoCrop Database) & ICAR Handbook of Agriculture  
**Publication:** FAO Plant Production & Protection Series / ICAR Agronomy Technical Monograph  
**Topics:** Bioclimatic Requirements, Thermal Limits, Rainfall Envelopes, Soil Suitability across Major Crop Groups  

## 1. Ecological Requirements of Major Crop Families

### A. Cereal & Grain Crops
- **Rice (*Oryza sativa*):** Requires high soil moisture ($\theta > 30\%$), heavy nitrogen demand (80–120 kg/ha), high humidity (>80%), warm temperatures (20–35°C), and substantial seasonal rainfall (>150–250 mm). Tolerates slightly acidic to neutral soils (pH 5.5–7.0). Heavy clay and clay loam soils with high water retention are ideal.
- **Maize (*Zea mays*):** Deep, well-drained fertile loams rich in nitrogen and organic matter. Optimal temperature: 18–27°C; moderate rainfall: 60–100 mm. Highly sensitive to waterlogging at early seedling stages; requires soil pH 6.0–7.2.

### B. Grain Legumes & Pulses (Nitrogen Fixers)
- **Chickpea, Lentil, Blackgram, Mungbean, Pigeonpeas, Kidneybeans:** Thrives in light to medium-textured well-drained soils. Moderate nitrogen requirements (20–40 kg N/ha starter) due to symbiotic $N_2$-fixation via *Rhizobium*. Requires moderate-to-high phosphorus (40–70 kg/ha) for root nodulation and ATP synthesis. Sensitive to waterlogging and salinity ($EC > 1.2\text{ dS/m}$). Optimal pH: 6.5–7.8.

### C. Fruit & Horticultural Crops
- **Apple (*Malus domestica*):** Temperate zone crop requiring low temperatures (15–24°C) with significant winter chilling hours. High soil phosphorus and potassium accumulator ($P > 100\text{ kg/ha}, K > 150\text{ kg/ha}$). Prefers well-drained slightly acidic to neutral soils (pH 5.5–6.5).
- **Banana (*Musa acuminata*):** Tropical perennial with massive vegetative canopy and heavy water and potassium uptake ($K > 150–200\text{ kg/ha}$, high humidity >75%, temperature 22–32°C). Requires rich, friable soils high in organic carbon.
- **Grapes (*Vitis vinifera*):** High demand for phosphorus and potassium. Prefers deep, well-drained sandy loams; moderate temperature 18–32°C. Sensitive to high rainfall during ripening (fruit splitting risk).

### D. Commercial & Fibre Crops
- **Cotton (*Gossypium hirsutum*):** Heavy feeder requiring high nitrogen ($N > 100\text{ kg/ha}$), deep black clay soils (Vertisols) with good moisture retention, warm sunny climate (22–35°C), and neutral to alkaline pH (6.5–8.0). High salinity tolerance relative to pulses.
- **Coffee (*Coffea arabica / robusta*):** Subtropical/tropical highland perennial requiring shaded environments, high rainfall (150–200 mm), moderate temperatures (20–28°C), and acidic, porous volcanic soils rich in humus (pH 5.5–6.5).
