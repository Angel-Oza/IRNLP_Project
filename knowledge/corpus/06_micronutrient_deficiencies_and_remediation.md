# ICAR Micronutrient Compendium: Soil Micronutrient Deficiencies and Agronomic Correction

**Source Authority:** Indian Council of Agricultural Research (ICAR) — All India Coordinated Research Project on Micro and Secondary Nutrients  
**Publication:** ICAR Micronutrients in Agriculture Bulletin / TNAU Agronomy Compendium  
**Topics:** Zinc (Zn), Iron (Fe), Boron (B), Manganese (Mn), Copper (Cu), Sulphur (S), Micronutrient Interactions  

## 1. Micronutrient Diagnosis and Critical Limits

Although required in minor quantities (parts per million, ppm), micronutrients govern enzyme catalytic sites, chlorophyll biosynthesis, and hormonal auxin balance:

### A. Zinc (Zn) — Critical Limit: 0.60 ppm (DTPA-Extractable)
- **Deficiency Symptoms:** "Khaira" disease in rice, white bud in maize, little leaf syndrome. Interveinal chlorosis on middle leaves with bronze/reddish-brown necrotic spotting.
- **Predisposing Conditions:** High soil pH (>7.5), calcareous soils, heavy phosphorus over-fertilization (antagonistic Zn-P precipitation in roots).
- **Remediation:** Soil application of Zinc Sulphate ($ZnSO_4 \cdot 7H_2O$) at 25 kg/ha at sowing, or foliar spray of 0.5% $ZnSO_4$ with 0.25% slaked lime during active tillering.

### B. Iron (Fe) — Critical Limit: 4.50 ppm (DTPA-Extractable)
- **Deficiency Symptoms:** Interveinal chlorosis appearing first on the youngest emergent leaves; veins remain sharply green while interveinal areas turn pale yellow or ivory white.
- **Predisposing Conditions:** High carbonate ($CaCO_3$) alkaline soils (lime-induced chlorosis), waterlogged calcareous soils.
- **Remediation:** Foliar spray of 1.0% Ferrous Sulphate ($FeSO_4$) + 0.1% citric acid, or soil application of Fe-EDDHA chelates in alkaline soils.

### C. Boron (B) — Critical Limit: 0.50 ppm (Hot Water Soluble)
- **Deficiency Symptoms:** Death of terminal growing buds ("rosette" appearance), cracking of fruits, internal browning in cauliflower, poor pollen viability, and incomplete grain setting.
- **Remediation:** Soil application of Borax ($Na_2B_4O_7 \cdot 10H_2O$) at 10 kg/ha or foliar application of 0.2% Solubor at flower initiation.

### D. Sulphur (S) — Critical Limit: 10.0 ppm ($0.15\% \text{ CaCl}_2$)
- **Deficiency Symptoms:** Yellowing of youngest top leaves (unlike nitrogen which affects older lower leaves first), reduced oil synthesis in oilseeds (mustard, soybean, groundnut).
- **Remediation:** Soil application of Agricultural Gypsum ($CaSO_4 \cdot 2H_2O$) at 200–250 kg/ha, Single Super Phosphate (SSP containing 12% S), or elemental sulphur.
